How to swap PEPE to ETH: the complete walkthrough

  1. Pick the destination

    Open the dashboard. Select PEPE on Ethereum, enter your amount and choose ETH, USDT or USDC from the available routes.

  2. Check the whole quote

    Review the receiving amount, fees, minimum and payout network. Enter the matching receiving address and follow the order instructions.

  3. Send, then track

    Send the requested PEPE amount to the order’s deposit address. The service releases the receiving asset after your PEPE transaction confirms and the order is processed.

What fees apply when you swap PEPE to ETH?

Ethereum network fee
A standard PEPE transfer uses ETH for gas. Keep ETH in the sending wallet even when you are swapping PEPE into ETH.
Service fee and exchange rate
Compare the final receiving amount. Service fees, the quoted spread and route liquidity all affect what you receive.
Approval and payout costs
A wallet-based contract swap can require a separate token approval. Check whether the quote includes the receiving network fee.

PEPE swap stuck or failed? Fixes that work

Pending on Ethereum

Check the transaction hash first. Network demand or a low gas fee can delay inclusion. Use your wallet’s speed-up option if available; avoid sending a duplicate deposit.

Failed or quote expired

Check your ETH gas balance and refresh the quote. For contract swaps, review the token allowance and slippage. A reverted Ethereum transaction still consumes gas.

Confirmed, but no payout

Check the order status and confirmation requirement. Give the service’s support your order ID and transaction hash if processing has stalled.

Common questions about swapping PEPE

Where can I swap PEPE to ETH?

Open the dashboard, choose PEPE on Ethereum as the sending token and ETH as the receiving asset. Review the available quote and follow the deposit instructions.

Can I swap PEPE to USDT or USDC?

Select USDT or USDC from the available receiving assets. Match the payout network to your wallet or exchange deposit network before confirming the order.

Do I need ETH to swap PEPE?

Yes, a standard PEPE transfer from your Ethereum wallet needs ETH for gas. PEPE does not pay that network fee, even when ETH is the asset you will receive.

How long does it take to swap PEPE?

The total time includes Ethereum deposit confirmations, order processing and the receiving network’s confirmation time. Follow the estimate and status shown for your order.

Why does my swap PEPE quote change?

PEPE prices, route liquidity and network fees change. A floating quote follows the market until its rate is set. Refresh expired quotes and check the net receiving amount before sending.
